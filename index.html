Why RUSCA?

We’re the official undergraduate supply chain organization at Rutgers Business School. Our board partners with companies, alumni, and faculty to translate coursework into experiential learning.

  • Weekly programming spanning logistics, procurement, analytics, and ops
  • Alumni insight through the Supply Chain Central podcast and blog
  • Accelerated career pipelines via externships and corporate tours
Our Mission

Cultivating Supply Chain Leaders Since 2009

RUSCA is an undergraduate student organization dedicated to informing and engaging Rutgers students interested in Supply Chain Management through immersive learning and industry engagement.

From classroom concepts to industry impact

Founded alongside the Rutgers Business School Supply Chain Management program, RUSCA enriches academic learning with corporate spotlights, site visits, externships, and mentorship experiences. Members explore strategies in global sourcing, procurement, risk analysis, logistics, operations, sales planning, demand fulfillment, and continuous improvement—learning something new every time we meet.

As the discipline evolves, we stay on the leading edge with alumni and corporate partners who mentor students, surface internships and co-ops, and spark dialogue on the latest industry trends.

What We Do

Programs & Events that Power Scarlet Success

Signature initiatives and programming connect students with the people, insights, and experiences that make Rutgers supply chain education stand out.

Kickoff

General Interest Meetings (GIM)

Welcome new members, meet the executive board, and learn about committee placements, upcoming programs, and how to get involved from day one. Our bi-weekly GIMs feature skill-building workshops, resume and interview labs, and alumni mentorship sessions designed to prepare members for internships and full-time roles.

  • Held at the start of each semester and bi-weekly throughout
  • Overview of committees, leadership, and events
  • Career readiness workshops and professional development
Industry

Corporate Spotlights

Interactive sessions with partner companies highlighting supply chain careers, current projects, and pathways to internships and full-time roles. These events feature networking opportunities, breakout discussions, and direct access to Fortune 500 partners who collaborate with RUSCA for recruitment and experiential opportunities.

  • Featuring firms from logistics, retail, pharma, and tech
  • Networking and breakout discussions
  • Direct recruitment opportunities and career pathways
Experience

Externship Program

Immersive project-based learning that pairs students with corporate partners to solve supply chain challenges and present recommendations. This multi-week program provides hands-on experience with dedicated mentors, real-world problem-solving, and the opportunity to present final recommendations to partner leadership teams.

  • Multi-week engagements with dedicated mentors
  • Final presentations to partner leadership teams
  • Real-world problem-solving and skill development
Immersion

Site Visits

On-location tours of distribution centers, innovation labs, and fulfillment operations that bring coursework to life and spark career ideas. These immersive experiences include Q&A sessions with operations and logistics leaders, providing insights into real-world supply chain operations.

  • Travel to partner facilities across the tri-state area
  • Q&A with operations and logistics leaders
  • Behind-the-scenes access to real supply chain operations
Community

Annual Banquet & Awards

A year-end celebration recognizing member achievements, announcing externship partners, and honoring graduates entering the supply chain workforce. This event celebrates leadership, service, and academic honors while providing photo opportunities with sponsors and alumni.

  • Leadership, service, and academic honors
  • Photo moments with sponsors and alumni
  • Committee placements and project ownership opportunities
Development

Leadership Development

Committee placements, project ownership, and executive board roles that cultivate confident and capable student leaders. Members gain leadership experience through year-round community service, peer mentoring, and organizational responsibilities that build professional skills.

  • Committee roles and project ownership
  • Executive board leadership opportunities
  • Year-round community service initiatives
